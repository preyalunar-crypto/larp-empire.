So, what is LARP?

Step inside the story.

LARP means Live Action Role-Playing. People create characters and bring stories to life through acting, costumes, imagination and teamwork.

You could be a royal advisor, a travelling explorer, a forest guardian or a mysterious wizard. You decide who your character is and how their story unfolds.

Need to know

Quick questions

Do I need experience to try LARP?

No. You can begin with a character idea and a simple story. If you join an in-person group, check its age guidance, rules and safety arrangements first.

Do I need an expensive costume?

No. A character can start with everyday clothes and a few creative details. Imagination matters more than expensive outfits.

Does my progress save?

Yes. This version automatically saves your character, badges, story ending, challenge count and quiz best score in your browser. Use the backup button if you want to move it.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers. Your saved progress is separate in each browser unless you export and import a backup.
